🏒 Ice Hockey Guide for Dummies!

🥅 GAME STRUCTURE

Ice hockey is a fast-paced team sport where six players per side use sticks to shoot a hard rubber puck into the opposing team's net.

⛸️ REQUIRED GEAR

  • Helmets & facemasks
  • Skates
  • Hockey stick
  • Protective Equipment

⭐️ REQUIRED SKILLS

Dummy must master skating prior to starting ice hockey. They can practice passing and game IQ once they start the sport.
